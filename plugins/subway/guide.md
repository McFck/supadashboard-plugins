A Japanese-style passenger information display for your dashboard. A train runs its line, announces stations and shows the route map. Nothing needs to be set up.

## Use it

1. In **Layout**, add the **Subway** widget.
2. Pick the line and stations in the **Route** section of this page, the display languages in **Languages**, and the chimes and announcements in **Sound**.
3. Switch between the **Display**, **Map** and **Run** views from the widget's control strip, or with the **Change Subway view** action.

## Buttons and scenarios

- Actions: **Change Subway line**, **Subway emergency stop**, **Suspend Subway service**, **Resume Subway service** and **Toggle Subway immersion**.
- Scenarios can react to **Train departed**, **Train arrived**, **Run state changed**, **Emergency stop** and **Service suspended**.
- The **Subway mixer volume** variable sets how loud the Subway plays.
