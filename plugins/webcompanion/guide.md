Controls Chrome, Edge or another Chromium browser from the dashboard: switch, close and reorder tabs, move the mouse pointer and scroll pages with a touchpad, open websites and control video and audio. It talks only to SupaDashboard on this PC.

It has two parts: this plugin and the WebCompanion browser extension.

## 1. Install

1. Install this plugin from the Market.
2. Install the WebCompanion extension from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/supadashboard-webcompanion/TODO). In Edge, allow extensions from other stores when asked.
3. Without the store: download the [extension folder](https://github.com/McFck/supadashboard-plugins/tree/main/plugins/webcompanion/extension), open `chrome://extensions`, turn on **Developer mode**, click **Load unpacked** and pick the folder.
4. Pin the extension: click the puzzle icon in the browser toolbar, then the pin next to **SupaDashboard WebCompanion**.

## 2. Pair

1. In the Connection section of this page, click **Generate code**.
2. Click the WebCompanion icon in the browser and type the six digits. The code works for two minutes.
3. Status changes to **Paired** in both places. Pairing is kept after restarts.

## 3. Use it

In **Layout**, add the widgets:

- **Browser Touchpad**
  - Slide one finger to move the pointer. Tap to click.
  - Hold one finger, then slide to drag, for example to select text.
  - Slide two fingers up or down to scroll the page.
  - Swipe two fingers left or right to switch to the next or previous tab.
- **Browser Tabs**: every open tab, with pinned tabs and tab groups.
  - Tap a tab to switch to it, or its X to close it.
  - Tap a group's name to collapse or expand it.
  - Hold a tab, then drag it to move it within its group.
  - Flick to scroll through long lists.
  - Choose **Vertical** or **Horizontal** in the widget's settings.

Buttons can also open a website, switch and reopen tabs, go back, reload, scroll, and play, pause or seek video and audio.

## If it does not connect

- Keep SupaDashboard running: the extension shows **Dashboard offline** without it.
- **Pair the browser again** means the pairing was removed: generate a new code.
- **Update extension** or **Update SupaDashboard** means the two versions do not match.
